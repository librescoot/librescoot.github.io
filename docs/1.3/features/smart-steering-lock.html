Smart Steering Lock

The handlebar lock releases at the left stop and engages there after shutdown.

How it works

When you power on the scooter, move the handlebars to the left stop. The handlebar lock releases with an audible click.

After shutdown

After shutdown, you have about 60 seconds to move the handlebars to the left stop. The handlebar lock engages as soon as they reach that position.
