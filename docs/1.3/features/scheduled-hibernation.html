Timed and Scheduled Hibernation

Let the scooter sleep when you do and wake automatically when you need it again.

Choose a duration

Start Hibernation from the companion app and choose when the scooter should wake. Common durations and a custom choice are available, up to seven days by default.

Set a recurring schedule

Create a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule in the app. Choose when Hibernation starts and when the scooter should wake again. The schedule is stored on the scooter, so the phone does not need to stay connected.

What to expect

Scheduled Hibernation starts only after the scooter is locked in standby. If you are still riding or the scooter is unlocked at the chosen time, it waits for standby without moving the planned wake time. Its clock must have synchronized before a schedule can run; GPS provides the time without a SIM.
