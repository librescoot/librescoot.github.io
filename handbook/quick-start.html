Quick start

Shortcuts for everyday use: opening the menu, the riding shortcut menu, Bluetooth updates, Update Mode for maps and recovery, and entering and saving navigation destinations. The full day-to-day flow is on Riding.

Opening the menu

In park mode, open the on-display menu with two short pulls on the left brake lever in quick succession. Inside the menu, tap the left brake to scroll and hold it to go back; tap the right brake to select or confirm.

1.3+ Holding the right brake runs the highlighted row's primary action when one is available. Holding the left brake for three seconds closes the menu from any level. The hints at the bottom reflect the current level and any available action.

More on The display → Opening the menu.

Shortcut menu while riding

While riding, a compact shortcut menu is available, operated entirely from the seatbox switch on the handlebar. That way you don't have to take a hand off the bars.

  1. Hold the seatbox switch. After about 0.8 seconds the shortcut menu appears and starts cycling through its entries every ¾ second on its own.
  2. Once the entry you want is highlighted, release the switch. Your selection stays marked for three seconds.
  3. Within those three seconds, briefly press the seatbox switch again to run the action.

A double short tap on the seatbox switch toggles the hazard lights directly, without going through the menu.

More on The display → Shortcut menu while riding.

Firmware updates over Bluetooth

1.2+ For normal updates within your current channel, use a delta from stasis for unu (Android on Google Play, iOS on TestFlight) over Bluetooth whenever one is available. It needs no SIM card, cable, or Update Mode and is the preferred path for routine firmware updates. The scooter must already be running Librescoot 1.2 or newer.

More on Updates.

Maps, recovery and updates over USB

Use Update Mode for maps and routing data, recovery, an offline channel change, or a firmware update without a suitable Bluetooth delta. You don't need a SIM card in the scooter, and you don't have to open the housing – just a USB Mini-B cable:

  1. Power the scooter up and open the menu.
  2. Pick Settings → System → Update Mode….
  3. Connect your laptop to the scooter's USB Mini-B port. It mounts as a removable drive; copy the files, eject the drive cleanly, and then leave Update Mode.

Which files go where (map tiles, routing tiles, .mender updates) is described on USB File Transfer (Update Mode).

Entering and saving a navigation destination

Navigation is set up directly on the parked scooter, no phone needed. The riding shortcut menu does not offer destination entry:

  1. Open the menu, go to Navigation.
  2. Pick one of the three sources:
    • Enter Address – pick city, then street, then (optionally) house number from the address registry on the scooter.
    • Recent Destinations – a list of places you've ridden to recently.
    • Saved Locations – save your own places and recall them later, like "home" or your current location before a trip.
  3. Put the scooter in drive mode. The speedometer switches to the map view and navigation runs.

The first time around, map and routing data have to be installed. If navigation isn't responding, go through the previous section and copy the right packages onto the scooter via Update Mode.
