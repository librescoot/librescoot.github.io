Integrations

Librescoot is open: the firmware on the scooter, the phone apps, and the Home Assistant integration come from separate projects. This page lists what exists, what each one needs, and who maintains it.

The label says how a project talks to your scooter

Bluetooth means the connection runs directly between the scooter and your device, with no SIM card and no cloud. Sunshine means the project needs the scooter to be online and a Sunshine account.

Apps for your phone

Control, vehicle status, and firmware updates in your hand. The first two apps are builds of the same application.

stasis for unu

by freal

Bluetooth

The app that replaces the original unu app. It was built independently of the Librescoot project, but it works with Librescoot scooters and it is the app most people here run.

  • Lock, seatbox, blinkers, and horn
  • Battery, range, odometer, and vehicle status
  • Install firmware updates over Bluetooth (Librescoot 1.2 and newer)
  • Send destinations to the scooter's navigation

Librescoot App for unu

by librescoot

Bluetooth

The same app with Librescoot branding, tracking the original closely. It carries its own identifier (org.librescoot.mobile.unu), so it installs alongside the original app instead of replacing it.

  • Same feature set as stasis for unu
  • Nightly builds as an APK on GitHub
  • Prereleases through TestFlight and the internal Play track

Librescoot Navigation

by ligustah

Sunshine

An Android app that relays a shared destination from your maps app to the scooter. You share a place the way you normally would and the scooter navigates there. There are no ready-made builds yet; it is built from source.

  • Receives Google and Apple Maps links as a share target
  • Sets and clears the scooter's current destination
  • Talks to Sunshine; base URL and API token live in its settings

Home Assistant

Two ways to get the scooter into Home Assistant: over Sunshine when it is online, or over Bluetooth when it has no SIM card.

Sunshine Scooter

by librescoot

Sunshine

Brings the scooter in through your Sunshine account. The scooter has to be online for this, either over cellular directly or through a cloud client such as radio-gaga. Installs as a custom repository in HACS.

  • Location as a device tracker on the map
  • Sensors for battery, range, speed, motor, signal quality, and firmware version
  • Switches for lock and alarm, plus blinkers, horn, sounds, and locate
  • Second battery, AUX battery, and CBB battery where present
  • Odometer as a long-term statistic

Librescoot-BLE-Component

by dtill

Bluetooth

1.4+ For scooters without an LTE contract. An ESP32-S3 next to the scooter talks to it over Bluetooth, and this integration adds pairing, firmware updates, and scooter discovery in Home Assistant. Readings and the alarm keep working while the scooter hibernates.

  • Pairing through a repair: type the code from the scooter's dashboard
  • Home Assistant fetches firmware updates and hands them to the ESP
  • Scooters several releases behind update in one install and one reboot
  • Bluetooth presence sensor shows whether the scooter is at home
  • Needs the librescoot-esphome component on an ESP32-S3

Build your own

If you want to build something for the scooter yourself, you do not need to touch the firmware: the scooter keeps its state in Redis and takes commands over the same paths that lsc and the apps use.
